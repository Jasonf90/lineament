# Lineament Support

Email **[jfdevios@gmail.com](mailto:jfdevios@gmail.com)**. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and the app version (Settings › About › App version), and what happened. Please don't send face photos or data exports: we don't need them to help, and we don't keep a copy of your data.

We can't give medical, skin or mental health advice. For anything about your health, talk to a doctor, dermatologist or pharmacist.

## If you're struggling

If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call your local emergency number now. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). In the app, "Feeling preoccupied with your looks?" lists places to talk to someone, and "Hide scores for a while" keeps your routine without numbers.

## Scans

**The scan won't capture.** Face a soft, even light (a window in front of you works well), hold the iPhone at eye level about an arm's length away, keep your face centered and level, and keep a neutral expression. The guide tells you which one thing to adjust.

**A category says "Not enough signal".** Lineament doesn't guess. Hair and grooming need a hair mask that only some front cameras provide, and never for imported photos. Skin is skipped in very uneven or tinted light, or when a smoothing filter seems to be on.

**My score is close to 55.** That's the middle of Lineament's scale: a scan at every typical value reads 55, and the typical range runs from 45 to 65. See [How Lineament rates a scan](methodology).

**My score changed between two scans.** Light, angle, expression, distance and grooming all move results. Lineament compares scans only when the photos are comparable, and shows "No meaningful change" for small differences.

## Subscriptions

**Restore Pro on a new iPhone.** Settings › Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account.

**Redeem an offer code.** Settings › Redeem code.

**Manage or cancel.** iOS Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period or trial ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

**Ask for a refund.** Apple handles payments and refunds. Go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), sign in and choose the purchase.

## Your data

**Where is my data?** On your iPhone only. Your face photos and analysis stay on this iPhone in version 1. There's no Lineament account to sign in to. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).

**Moving to a new iPhone.** Version 1 excludes its data from iCloud and device backups, so your scans and routine don't move to a new iPhone. Export my data saves a copy for your records, but it can't be imported back into the app.

**Delete a scan, photos or everything.** Settings › Privacy and your data. You can delete a scan, delete photos only, withdraw facial-analysis consent, or delete all data.

**Use Lineament without photos.** Choose "Start without a photo" on the welcome screen.

## Accessibility

Lineament supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type up to the largest accessibility sizes, Reduce Motion and Increase Contrast. If something is hard to use, email us and tell us which setting you use.
