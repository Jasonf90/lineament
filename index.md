# Lineament

Lineament for iPhone turns a guided front-facing scan into an explainable estimate and a short daily plan for what you can change: skin care, grooming, hair, sleep and posture, style, and how you take photos. Your face photos and analysis stay on this iPhone in version 1.

Lineament is for adults 18 or older. Results are estimates, not medical advice.
