# Lineament Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 30 October 2026 · **Applies to:** Lineament for iPhone, version 1, and these web pages

## The short version

Your face photos and analysis stay on this iPhone in version 1.

- There is no Lineament account and no Lineament server. The app doesn't upload your photos, your facial measurements or your ratings.
- There are no ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs in the app.
- Lineament's photos, analysis and database are excluded from iCloud and device backups in version 1.
- You can delete a scan, delete photos only, withdraw consent, or delete all data at any time, in the app.
- We don't sell or share your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

A few things do involve Apple or leave the app because you ask them to. They're explained under "What can leave the iPhone".

## Who we are

Lineament is made by its developer, the seller named on Lineament's App Store page. In this policy "we", "us" and "our" mean that developer. You can reach us at [jfdevios@gmail.com](mailto:jfdevios@gmail.com).

## Face data

Face data in Lineament means facial landmarks (points around your eyes, brows, nose, mouth and jaw) and the measurements and ratings calculated from them. Some laws, for example in Illinois, Texas and Washington, and the EU and UK data protection laws, treat measurements of face geometry as biometric data.

- **Purpose.** Lineament uses face data only to give you the analysis you asked for, on your iPhone. It isn't used to identify you or anyone else, and Lineament doesn't recognize faces.
- **No disclosure.** We never receive your face data. We don't sell, lease, trade, share or disclose it, and we don't use it for advertising, profiling or data mining.
- **Retention and destruction.** Face data stays on your iPhone only until the first of these happens: you delete that scan, you withdraw consent, you delete all data, or you delete the app. Each of these permanently deletes it from the app. We hold no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing else to destroy.
- **Your own face only.** Scan only your own face. The consent screen asks you to confirm that the face is yours and that you're 18 or older.

## What can leave the iPhone

- **Purchases (Apple).** Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app asks the App Store for plan prices when you open the subscription screen, and during a free trial if it needs the price for your trial reminder and doesn't have it saved. It also asks for your subscription status, and it redeems an offer code or restores purchases only when you tap Redeem code or Restore purchases. Apple's privacy policy applies to that. We don't receive your payment details. Apple gives developers App Store sales and subscription reports.
- **Exports you choose to share.** Settings › Export my data creates a ZIP of your ratings, measurements, routine and check-ins. Photos are off by default; if you include them, the app warns you that the file will contain face photos. You choose where the file goes with the iOS share sheet, and what happens to it after that depends on where you send it.
- **Share cards you choose to share.** Progress › a month › Share makes an image on the iPhone: a line drawing from your scan's points (never the photo), your style words and palette, or your routine days. It never shows a score. Nothing leaves until you pick where it goes in the iOS share sheet, or save it to Photos. A shared card may include a link to Lineament's App Store page.
- **Links you tap.** Terms of Use, this Privacy Policy and Support open in Safari, outside the app.
- **Apple diagnostics.** If you've chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide crash reports to us under Apple's terms. That sharing is controlled by you and Apple, not by Lineament.

Lineament doesn't use iCloud, CloudKit or any cloud storage in version 1.

## Logs

Lineament writes technical logs to the iPhone's system log to help troubleshoot problems. They stay on the iPhone. Values in them are redacted by default, and they never contain images, landmark coordinates, measurements, scores, file contents, purchase transaction IDs or anything you type.

## Consent, and withdrawing it

Before your first scan, Lineament shows a facial-analysis consent screen that's separate from the Terms of Use. Analysis starts only after you tick "I agree to facial analysis as described above". The app records which version of the consent you agreed to and when, on the iPhone. If the consent text changes, the app asks you again before your next scan.

**Withdraw consent** in Settings › Privacy and your data. Analysis stops. After you confirm, the app deletes your photos, thumbnails, facial landmarks, measurements and ratings. Your routine, goals and settings are kept. You can keep using the routine without photos, and a new scan asks for consent again.

Where the EU or UK data protection laws apply, facial analysis relies on your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time, and the rest of the app's processing is what's needed to provide the features you ask for.

## Health-related answers

The weekly check-in asks how your skin felt, how you slept and how your energy was, each on a scale of 1 to 5. Some laws, such as Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law, treat answers like these as consumer health data. In Lineament:

- They're used only to show your own answers next to your trend. They never change your score.
- They stay in the app's protected storage on your iPhone, excluded from backups. We don't collect or receive them, and we don't sell or share them with anyone.
- They're included in an export only if you create one, and they're deleted with Delete all data or when you delete the app.

Lineament never infers health, mood or tiredness from your face.

## Your choices and controls

All of these are in the app, under Settings:

- **Delete a scan:** removes that scan's photos and thumbnails and everything calculated from them.
- **Delete photos only:** removes every photo and thumbnail and keeps your ratings, measurements and routine.
- **Withdraw consent:** described above.
- **Delete all data:** removes every photo, thumbnail, landmark, measurement, rating, routine, check-in, usage event, preference and pending reminder, and any temporary export files. Your App Store subscription isn't affected by this; manage or cancel it in iOS Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.
- **Export my data:** described above. Photos are off by default.
- **Goals and answers:** change your goals and the answers your plan is built from.
- **Use Lineament without photos:** choose "Start without a photo" on the welcome screen to get a routine with no camera use and no face analysis.

Deleting the app also removes everything it stored on the iPhone.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK, California or another US state with a privacy law), you may have the right to know what personal data is processed about you, to get a copy of it, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to its use, to withdraw consent, and not to be treated differently for using these rights.

Because Lineament keeps your data on your iPhone and we don't receive it, you can use these rights yourself, straight away, in the app: Export my data gives you a copy, Delete a scan, Delete photos only and Delete all data delete it, Withdraw consent stops analysis, and Goals and answers lets you correct what your plan uses. We can't see, export or delete data on your iPhone for you, because we don't have it.

If you have a question or a request about your data, email [jfdevios@gmail.com](mailto:jfdevios@gmail.com). We'll reply within 30 days and won't ask for more information than we need to answer you. If you're in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## How long data is kept

On the iPhone until you delete it, delete all data, or delete the app. Usage events expire automatically after 90 days. Temporary export files are deleted when sharing finishes and cleared at each launch. Face data follows the schedule under "Face data" above.

## Where data is processed

Lineament processes your data on your iPhone, wherever you are. We don't transfer it to other countries, because we don't receive it. Apple handles purchases under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Security

Photos and thumbnails use iOS Data Protection's strongest class, so they can't be read while the iPhone is locked. The database is protected the same way, except that it can finish writing if the iPhone locks mid-save. Notifications never show scores or analysis on the Lock Screen, widgets show only task titles and your streak, and the app covers its screen in the App Switcher. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so keep your iPhone protected with a passcode.
